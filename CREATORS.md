# Creator mentions

Paying small study creators for a short mention of Grasp in one of their
videos, with a pinned comment linking to graspstudy.com.
Started 2026-10-27, at the user's request, after paid ads brought visitors but
almost no signups (Meta: 99 visitors, 0 signups; Google: 34 visitors, 1 signup,
0 paid). Revised 2026-11-07: the first offer was free Max for an honest video;
the user changed it to a paid mention, since a free plan asked more of the
creator than a short mention and gave Grasp less to show for it.

## Who to look for

- **Uni students who post study content**, 5k to 50k followers, on TikTok,
  Instagram or YouTube. Med, engineering, law and "study with me" accounts
  most of all. Small accounts are more likely to answer a direct message and
  to take a small fee.
- **US and Canadian first.** Their semester runs to December; Australian
  lectures have ended and exams are on. Australian creators come back into
  play for late January to March (O-Week, new timetables).
- **Not under 18.** Grasp is for 13 and over, but a paid deal is a business
  arrangement; keep to adults. Skip anyone whose posts are mostly high school.
- **Check before messaging:** recent posts (active in the last two weeks),
  comments that are real people asking questions, and that they have shown an
  app before without it reading as an advert.

How to find them: search TikTok and Instagram for "lecture notes", "study with
me uni", "med school notes", "engineering student", "how I take notes in
lectures", and look at who comments on bigger study creators' posts.

## What to offer

**US$50 to US$100 per creator**, for a short mention of Grasp in one of their
videos (a real lecture or study session, where they show or mention Grasp),
plus a **pinned comment** on that video with the link. Set 2026-11-07 by the
user. Start with one or two creators and check what the post brings before
offering more.

- **The mention:** a few seconds in a video they were already making. No
  script. They can say what they think, good or bad.
- **The pinned comment:** the tagged link below, so `/admin/analytics` can
  follow signups to the creator.
- **Disclosure is required.** The post must be labelled as a paid partnership
  (TikTok's "Paid partnership" toggle, or #ad on Instagram). Both platforms
  and the consumer law in Australia and the US require it.
- **Payment:** pay after the post is live, not before. Send an invoice; for
  US payees, check what tax form they need before paying.
- **Do not** promise a result, a follower count or that Grasp will stay free.

## Their link

Each creator gets their own tagged link, which `/admin/analytics` follows to a
signup and a paid plan:

```
https://graspstudy.com/?utm_source=creator&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=<handle>
```

Use the creator's handle, lower case, for `<handle>`.

## Free promotion codes (no longer the offer)

Checkout still accepts Stripe promotion codes (`allow_promotion_codes` in
`createCheckoutSession`), set up on 2026-10-27 for free-Max deals. The coupon is
"Creator: free Max for 3 months", 100% off, repeating 3 months, with
`TESTCREATOR` in the sandbox and `GRASPTEST1` and `GRASPTEST2` (1 redemption
each) in the live account. Creator codes would go on that live coupon. Not
used for the paid mention; keep it for a later deal if one is wanted. A code
covering the whole charge asks for no card, and the plan ends when the code
does.

## What paid creators cost (2026-10-27)

Published 2026 benchmarks (Later, Hootsuite, HireInfluence; US dollars, and
they disagree) put a 50k-follower creator at about **US$500 to US$2,500 a
post**, about A$750 to A$3,800, with a dedicated YouTube video at the top of
that or above. Engagement, niche and usage rights move the price more than the
follower count. US$50 to US$100 is well below that, so it is a starting test
for small creators, not a market rate.
